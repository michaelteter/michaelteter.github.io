🇹🇭 Thailand Credit Card Facts

  • Bank of Thailand (BOT) Cap: Max interest rate on credit cards is legally capped at 16% APR.
  • Personal / Cash Loans: Revolving cash card rates can reach 25% APR.
  • Minimum Payment Rule: Bank of Thailand requires 8% (2024) to 10% (2025+) of balance, or minimum ฿500.
  • 0% Installment Promos: Thai stores frequently offer "0% for 10 months". This is safe only if you pay on time and don't miss a single due date!

💡 Smart Rules for Young Cardholders

  • Rule 1: Always treat a credit card like a debit card—only spend money you already have in the bank.
  • Rule 2: Always choose "Pay Statement in Full" to avoid paying a single baht of interest.
  • Rule 3: Never take cash advances from a credit card; interest starts calculating immediately from day 1 with no grace period!
  • Rule 4: Missed payments ruin your credit history (National Credit Bureau in Thailand).
