🇹🇭 Bank of Thailand (BOT) Facts

  • Interest Rate Cap: Credit card interest is strictly capped at 16% APR by Thai law.
  • Revolving Cash Advances: Cash cards and personal loan rates can reach up to 25% APR.
  • Minimum Payment Rule: Bank of Thailand requires 8% to 10% of total balance, with a mandatory floor of ฿500.
  • 0% Installment Promos: Thai mall promotions ("0% for 10 months") are safe only if you pay every month on time without missing a due date!

💡 Smart Financial Habits

  • Rule 1: Always treat a credit card like a debit card—never spend money you do not already have in the bank.
  • Rule 2: Always choose "Pay Statement Balance in Full" to avoid giving even 1 baht of interest to the bank.
  • Rule 3: Beware of debt stacking—multiple small monthly payments quickly eat your entire salary.
  • Rule 4: Late or missed payments harm your National Credit Bureau (NCB) score in Thailand.
